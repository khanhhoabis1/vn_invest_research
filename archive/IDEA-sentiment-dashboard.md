A sentiment-driven stock insight dashboard that helps retail investors make informed decisions using news and market data.

- Aggregate real-time financial news and social media posts for targeted stock tickers.
- Perform NLP sentiment scoring to generate daily buy/hold/sell signals.
- Provide a simulated portfolio tracker to test strategies without real capital.
- Display interactive charts comparing sentiment trends against historical prices.
- Send email alerts when a tracked stock's sentiment shifts significantly.
